## The brief

Disney appointed me to lead development of the **ESPN PlayStation 4** app: a sports streaming experience that had to perform well on console hardware and pass Sony's platform compliance checks.

## What I did

- **Led the ESPN PS4 app** end to end.
- **Built PlayStation 4/3 apps** for CBS, NHL and WWE in the same period.
- **Led a team of 4–5 engineers** and mentored junior developers.
- **Contributed to our in-house JavaScript/XML framework** for building Sony Trilithium apps.

## The outcome

Apps for four major sports and entertainment brands shipped on PlayStation, meeting Sony's performance, stability and compliance requirements.
