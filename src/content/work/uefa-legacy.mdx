## The brief

UEFA wanted a rich, media-heavy **Legacy website**. I first contributed to the project as a developer, then took over as lead.

## What I did

- **Led development** with a team of 3–4 engineers and mentored junior developers.
- **Project-managed the delivery**, coordinating UEFA stakeholders and the technical teams.

## The outcome

The project's success **secured a contract extension with UEFA**, at a very uncertain time for the company.
