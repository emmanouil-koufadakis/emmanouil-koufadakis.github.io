## The role

UK broadcasters expect their Roku apps to be reliable, quick and consistent with their brand on every device. As **Lead Roku Developer** I owned the full lifecycle for the BBC and Channel 5 Roku work: architecture, development, code quality and production releases.

## What I did

- **Led development** of the Roku applications, from technical design to release.
- **Wrote the team's Roku documentation on code conventions and best coding practices.** It became the standard onboarding resource for every new Roku developer joining the team.
- **Set design and coding standards** so the codebase stayed consistent as the team grew.
- **Mentored junior developers** in the Roku team.

## The outcome

Two broadcaster apps delivered to production, and a Roku team with shared standards and documentation that made onboarding new developers much faster.
