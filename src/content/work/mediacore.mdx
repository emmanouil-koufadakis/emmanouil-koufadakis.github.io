## The brief

MediaCore needed a Windows tablet app that let universities **record lectures and upload the media** to their server simply and reliably.

## What I did

- **Led and delivered** the MediaCore Windows application.
- **Led a team of 5–6 engineers** building Windows and UWP apps, including work for Camelot and HowStuffWorks at the same time.
- **Mentored junior developers** in the Windows team.

## The outcome

The app's success **secured Microsoft investment** to fund Phase 2 of MediaCore.
